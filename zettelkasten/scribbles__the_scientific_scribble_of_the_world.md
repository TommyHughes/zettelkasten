| 9     |          | For example, let's look at the Physicist's "dynamical law". These are the so-called laws of motion: Newtonian, Schrödinger Equation, Least Action.<br><br>But what *is* a law really? Suppose I kick a rock. It tumbles. Why? Why does it move at all?
